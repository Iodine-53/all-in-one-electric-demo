Maybe — and anyone who quotes you without checking is guessing. A Level 2 charger adds a continuous 40–60 amp load. We run a NEC load calculation on your existing service first. If your panel has the headroom, we install the dedicated 240V circuit. If it doesn't, we'll show you the math and price the upgrade honestly.

Florida sees more lightning strikes than any other state, and a single nearby strike can send thousands of volts through your service drop — frying HVAC boards, appliances, and anything with a chip. A panel-mounted surge protective device clamps that spike before it reaches your home. It's one of the cheapest insurance policies your electrical system can have.

Homes wired between roughly 1965 and 1973 often have aluminum branch circuits, which expand and contract more than copper — loosening connections over time and creating heat at outlets and switches. The fix isn't always a full rewire: we remediate with approved aluminum-to-copper connections at every device, which is the industry-accepted repair.
